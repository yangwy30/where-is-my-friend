## What's New — English (U.S.)

• Choose start and end dates in one calendar.
• Explore friends’ shared future plans, clearer city/state labels, and new city illustrations.
• Members can leave Trips; creators can remove members and delete shared trips.
• Get flight-planning reminders around 9 AM local time, or gently remind a trip member to add an outbound flight. Reminders can be turned off per trip.
• Enjoy quieter syncing and improvements to account switching, privacy, and notification reliability.
