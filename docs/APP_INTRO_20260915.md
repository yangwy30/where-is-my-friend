## 简短介绍

Across Us，让分散在不同城市的朋友，多一点见面的机会。

看看朋友最近在哪座城市，发现你们即将重叠的旅行日期；一起出发时，把大家的航班安排放在同一个行程里。从“你也在这里”，到“那我们见一面”。

### 描述

朋友散落在不同城市，见面的机会有时只差一句：“原来你也在。”

Across Us 帮你与朋友分享最近所在的城市，提前发现未来旅行中的相聚机会，也让共同出行的航班安排更清楚。

看看朋友在哪座城市

• 双方接受好友请求后，查看彼此共享的最近城市与更新时间。
• 用城市地标和朋友卡片，轻松浏览散落各地的朋友。
• 当你们最近共享的城市相同时，查看“Here together”同城提示；也可以选择开启同城通知。

提前发现下一次相聚

• 添加未来要去的城市与日期，无需先建立共同 Trip 或填写航班。
• 每份个人计划默认仅自己可见，由你选择分享给哪些好友。
• 当双方相互分享的计划在同一城市、日期重叠时，在“Together soon”查看相聚日期。
• 按需开启提醒，或通过系统分享菜单发出一句问候。

把共同出行放在一起

• 创建多个 Trips，按正在进行、即将出发和过去的行程整理。
• 邀请朋友加入，集中查看目的地、日期与成员。
• 每位成员添加和管理自己的航班；通过航线地图和到达看板查看出行安排。
• 查询航班信息，并按需开启该行程的航班变化提醒。

把朋友放在主屏幕

• 使用主屏幕与锁定屏幕小组件，快速查看朋友的城市状态。
• 选择想关注的朋友，并设置完整显示、隐藏姓名或全部隐藏。

分享的分寸，由你决定

• 通过 Apple 登录。
• 精确坐标在 iPhone 上用于判断城市，不上传连续 GPS 轨迹。
• 可以手动设置城市，也可以授权设备更新城市。
• 随时暂停城市共享，单独调整好友权限，或移除、屏蔽好友。
• 个人未来计划和共同 Trips 分别管理；加入 Trip 不会自动公开你的个人计划。

当前城市代表最近一次共享的更新；未来同城提示基于双方分享的计划。后台城市更新与通知受 iOS、网络及权限设置影响。航班信息为周期更新，出行请以航空公司信息为准。暂停城市共享不会自动撤回已分享的计划或行程内容。

## App Store English (U.S.)

### Subtitle

More chances to meet

### Promotional text

See your friends' latest cities, find dates when your travel plans overlap, and bring everyone's flights into one shared trip. Choose what to share and with whom.

### Description

Your friends are in different cities. Your next chance to meet might be closer than you think.

Across Us helps you share your latest city, discover when future travel plans overlap, and keep flights together when you travel as a group.

FRIENDS, CITY BY CITY

• See the latest city and update time shared by mutually accepted friends.
• Browse familiar faces and illustrated city landmarks.
• Find Here together moments when your recent shared cities match, with optional same-city notifications.

FIND YOUR NEXT CHANCE TO MEET

• Add a future city and travel dates without creating a group trip or adding a flight.
• Keep each personal plan private by default, then choose which friends can see it.
• Discover Together soon dates when plans you have shared with each other overlap in the same city.
• Choose reminders or open a greeting in the system share sheet.

TRAVEL TOGETHER, FROM DIFFERENT PLACES

• Organize multiple Trips into ongoing, upcoming, and past journeys.
• Invite friends to a shared destination and date range.
• Let each member add and manage their own flights.
• See routes and arrival details together on a map and flight board.
• Look up flight information and choose flight-change alerts for each trip.

A GLANCE CLOSER

• Add Home Screen and Lock Screen Widgets for your friends' city updates.
• Choose preferred friends and show full details, hide names, or keep the Widget private.

SHARING ON YOUR TERMS

• Sign in with Apple.
• Precise coordinates are used on your iPhone to determine a city; the app does not upload a continuous GPS trail.
• Set your city manually or allow city updates from your device.
• Pause city sharing, adjust it for individual friends, or remove and block people.
• Manage personal future plans separately from group Trips. Joining a Trip does not automatically publish a personal plan.

Current cities reflect the latest shared update. Upcoming matches are based on mutually shared plans. Background city updates and notifications depend on iOS scheduling, connectivity, and permissions. Flight information is refreshed periodically; confirm travel details with your airline. Pausing city sharing does not automatically withdraw previously shared plans or trip content.
