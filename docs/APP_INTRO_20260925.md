## 简体中文

### 副标题

分享城市，发现下一次相聚

### 宣传文本

看看朋友的下一站，找到你们重叠的日期。分享城市和旅行计划，一起出发时，把大家的航班与抵达时间放在同一个行程里。

### 描述

朋友现在在哪，接下来又会去哪？

Across Us 帮你分享城市与旅行计划，发现刚好同城的机会。看看朋友的下一站，找到你们重叠的日期；一起出发时，把大家的航班和抵达时间放在同一个行程里。

看看朋友的现在与下一站

浏览好友分享的城市与更新时间。看到日历标记，点开好友，就能查看对方分享的旅行城市和日期。

提前发现见面的机会

添加你将去的城市与日期，选择要分享给哪些好友。在好友计划中看看大家接下来去哪；当彼此分享的计划在同一城市重叠时，查看共同的日期。

同城时，打个招呼

在 Here together 中发现与你同城的朋友，也可以开启同城提醒。通过系统分享菜单，发出一句问候。

一起旅行，把安排放在一起

创建共同 Trip，邀请朋友加入。在可展开的地图上，看看大家从不同城市出发、汇聚到同一站的航线。每个人管理自己的航班，一起查看抵达时间和谁还没添加航班。按需开启航班变化提醒，或给同行朋友发一个添加航班的提醒。

不打开 App，也能看一眼

把好友城市小组件放到主屏幕或锁定屏幕，选择你想关注的朋友与显示方式。

通过 Apple 登录。城市和每份计划分享给谁，由你选择；也可以随时暂停城市分享、修改计划的分享范围或删除计划。

城市信息以最近共享的更新为准。通知与后台更新需要相应的系统权限；航班安排请以航空公司信息为准。

## English (U.S.)

### Subtitle

More chances to meet

### Promotional text

See where friends are headed, find dates when your plans overlap, and bring everyone's flights together when you travel as a group.

### Description

Where are your friends now, and where are they headed next?

Across Us brings shared cities and travel plans together, so you can find more chances to meet. See a friend's next stop, discover dates when your plans overlap, and keep everyone's flights in one place when you travel together.

FRIENDS, HERE AND NEXT

Browse the latest cities and updates your friends have shared. A calendar badge points to a friend's travel plans. Tap their card to see the cities and dates they've shared with you.

FIND YOUR NEXT CHANCE TO MEET

Add a city and your travel dates, then choose which friends to share them with. Browse Friend plans to see where people are headed. When your mutually shared plans overlap in the same city, discover the dates you'll both be there.

SAME CITY. SAY HELLO.

Find friends in the same city with Here together, and choose same-city alerts. Open the system share sheet to send a greeting.

TRAVEL TOGETHER

Create a shared Trip and invite your friends. Expand the map to see everyone's routes come together from different cities. Each person manages their own flights, while everyone can see arrival times and who still needs to add a flight. Choose flight-change alerts or send a planning reminder to a fellow traveler.

FRIENDS, A GLANCE AWAY

Add city widgets to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Choose your preferred friends and how much detail to display.

Sign in with Apple. Choose who sees your city and each plan. You can pause city sharing, change a plan's audience or delete a plan anytime.

Cities reflect the latest shared update. Notifications and background updates require the relevant iOS permissions. Confirm flight details with your airline.
