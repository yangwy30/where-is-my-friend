UI and interaction review fixes — build 26.

Please check:
• Friends home: clear city status, a prominent first-friend invitation, and sharing descriptions that match the current settings.
• My travel plans and Shared trips: clearer names and navigation; copied dates are saved independently.
• New travel plans select all current friends by default. Deselect anyone before saving, then reopen the plan to confirm your choices are preserved. Existing plans keep their saved audience.
• Withdraw a pending friend invitation. After the recipient refreshes, the withdrawn request must no longer be accepted.
• Share a meetup invite opens the iOS share sheet. Cancelling must return to the app without a sent-message claim.
• Follow system appearance, larger text, flight-number entry, calendars, and saving a trip while the keyboard is open.
• Same-city history: the current visit is separate from previous visits; durations update while the screen stays open.
• Updated privacy summary and English/Chinese text.

Please report the iPhone model, iOS version, reproduction steps and screenshots. Do not include authentication codes or sensitive personal information.
