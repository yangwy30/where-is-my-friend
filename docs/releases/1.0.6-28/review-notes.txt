Across Us uses Sign in with Apple. Reviewers can create an account with their own Apple Account; there is no shared username/password. Location and notification permissions are optional. This build uses the production service with local demo mode disabled.

Review paths:
1. Sign in, then open You > City sharing. Select a city manually without granting location access, or use Apple location and geocoding services. Friends see a city and its last update. Precise device coordinates are not sent to our servers. Automatic background updates require the relevant iOS permission and remain subject to system scheduling.
2. For two-person features, sign in on two devices with different Apple Accounts. Add each other by username from Friends and accept the request. Pending sent invitations can be withdrawn. Empty friend lists remain navigable.
3. You > My travel plans creates a personal city/date plan. New plans select all current accepted, unblocked friends; deselect anyone before saving. Maximum audience: 100. Clear the audience for an owner-only plan. Existing plans keep their saved audiences; adding a friend does not expand old plans.
4. Selected friends can browse a plan by default. “Show in friends’ travel plans” controls full-plan browsing, separately from overlap matching with selected friends who also share eligible plans. Turning browsing off does not disable matching. Overlap reminders are separate and default off. Friends’ travel plans is reached from Friends, and overlaps appear in Together soon.
5. Shared trips is a separate tab. Create a trip, invite a friend, and add your own flight number and departure date. Members manage their own flights. Route maps show airport routes from verified flights, not device locations or live aircraft positions. Lookup may return no results; saving an unverified number does not invent a route or schedule. Copying trip dates to a personal plan creates an independent draft, and joining a trip does not publish a personal plan.
6. You > Notifications provides the notification overview and controls. Flight-change alerts default off. Flight-planning reminders can be disabled per trip. Background updates, flight checks and push delivery are not guaranteed to be immediate.
7. Add Friends, near and far or Here together widgets through iOS. Home Screen and rectangular/circular Lock Screen styles are supported. You > Widget & Lock Screen controls privacy. Preferred-friend selection is available where supported on Friends widgets.
8. You > Privacy & data explains stored data. You > Delete account removes the authentication account, account-linked personal plans, friendships, city-sharing records and notification devices, then clears local account caches. Participant names and previously added flights may remain in shared trips for other members; the confirmation explains this and advises removing your flights first if you do not want them retained.

City sharing and personal-plan/trip sharing have separate controls. Stored data also includes same-city events/stay periods, flight lookup requests and bounded operational diagnostics described in the public privacy policy. No address-book import, advertising or cross-app tracking is included.

Version 1.0.6 updates same-city presentation, plan audiences, sharing/invitation/notification controls, Widget navigation, unsaved-edit protection, flight dates/editing, Chinese localization and accessibility. It does not provide in-app chat or a Say hello message-sending feature.
