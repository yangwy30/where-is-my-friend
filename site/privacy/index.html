Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 16, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Across Us is designed to share a city, not a live trail. Precise coordinates are processed on your device to determine a city and are not uploaded to our server. We do not sell personal data, serve ads, track you across other companies’ apps or websites, or use third-party advertising analytics.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes the Across Us iOS app, its Widget, and its supporting beta services (collectively, the “Service”). Across Us is an independent beta operated by the app developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

2. Information we process

  • Account and profile: a Supabase user identifier connected to Sign in with Apple, your chosen display name and username, avatar color, and authentication session information. Apple may provide account information to Apple and Supabase as part of authentication; we do not ask for your address book.
  • City-level presence: the city and country code you choose or allow the app to determine, the update time, and whether the source was manual, foreground location, a significant-change update, or a visit. Precise coordinates used by iOS and on-device geocoding are not sent to our server.
  • In-app social graph and choices: friend requests, accepted friendships, blocks, favorites, city-sharing choices, per-friend access choices, notification preferences, and Widget privacy mode.
  • Notifications and device registration: a random installation identifier, APNs device token, app bundle/environment information, last-seen time, and delivery status. APNs tokens are encrypted at rest. If a same-city event is created, we process the relevant friends’ display names, city, event time, and notification delivery status.
  • Local app data: your Supabase session, the most recent app snapshot, pending offline changes, and accepted friends’ last-known city information used by the Widget. This data stays in the app’s storage, Keychain, or shared App Group container unless it is part of a request sent to the Service.

3. How we use information

We use information only to authenticate you; maintain profiles, friendships, blocks, and privacy choices; show city-level presence in the app and Widget; synchronize changes; create and deliver eligible same-city moments; prevent duplicate notifications; protect the Service; and diagnose failed operations.

4. When information is shared

  • Accepted friends: receive only the latest city-level presence and update time allowed by your global and per-friend sharing choices. They do not receive your precise coordinates.
  • Apple: provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Notification text may pass through APNs; you can hide notification previews in the app or iOS settings.
  • Supabase: provides authentication, database, server functions, and managed infrastructure for the beta Service.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or secure the Service.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Location, notifications, and background access

Location and notification permissions are optional and requested through iOS. You may enter a city manually. If you grant location access, the app can determine a city on-device; background city refresh opportunities are controlled by iOS and may be delayed. You can pause all city sharing, change per-friend access, disable background updates, disable notifications, or revoke permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

6. Retention

  • Your current city record replaces the previous current-city record. We do not build a precise-coordinate or route history.
  • Account, profile, friendship, preference, current-city, device, and same-city event records are retained while your account is active and are removed when account deletion completes.
  • Disabled notification registrations and interrupted deletion diagnostics are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for delivery reliability, security, or completing the request.
  • Deleted data may remain for a limited period in encrypted provider backups before those backups age out under the provider’s retention cycle.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your profile; pause city sharing; change access for a friend; remove or block people; change Widget privacy; and control location, background, and notification permissions.

To delete your account, open You → Delete account in the app and confirm. The Service deletes your Supabase Auth identity and associated profile, friendships, preferences, current city, device registrations, events, and local app/Widget cache. Because this beta does not retain an Apple refresh token, you may separately remove the app’s authorization from your Apple Account under Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple. If in-app deletion fails, contact us using the support link below.

8. Security and international processing

We use TLS in transit, server-side authorization, row-level database controls, encrypted APNs token storage, and Apple/Supabase session protections. No system is perfectly secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing where they live. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the beta changes. We will update the date above and provide additional notice in the app when a change materially affects your rights or how information is used.

For privacy requests or questions, visit Support or open a private-data-free issue at GitHub Issues. Do not post authentication tokens, precise locations, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月 16 日

隐私原则摘要

Across Us 的目标是分享城市，而不是追踪实时路线。精确坐标只在设备上用于判断城市，不会上传到服务器。我们不出售个人数据、不投放广告、不跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你，也不使用第三方广告分析服务。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 Across Us iOS App、小组件及其配套测试服务（统称“本服务”）。Across Us 是由 App 开发者独立运营的测试版产品（下称“我们”）。

2. 我们处理的信息

  • 账号和资料：与“通过 Apple 登录”关联的 Supabase 用户标识、你选择的显示名称和用户名、头像颜色以及身份验证会话信息。Apple 和 Supabase 会在登录过程中处理必要的账号信息；我们不会请求你的系统通讯录。
  • 城市级位置：你手动选择或允许 App 判断的城市、国家/地区代码、更新时间，以及手动、前台定位、显著位置变化或到访等更新来源。iOS 提供并在设备端用于地理编码的精确坐标不会发送到我们的服务器。
  • App 内好友关系与选择：好友请求、已接受的好友关系、屏蔽、收藏、城市共享选择、逐好友访问设置、通知偏好和小组件隐私模式。
  • 通知与设备注册：随机安装标识、APNs 设备令牌、App Bundle/环境信息、最近活跃时间和发送状态。APNs 令牌会加密存储。同城事件会处理相关好友的显示名称、城市、事件时间及通知发送状态。
  • 本地 App 数据：Supabase 会话、最近的 App 快照、待同步操作，以及小组件使用的已接受好友最近城市。这些数据保存在 App 存储、钥匙串或共享 App Group 中，除非它们属于发往本服务的请求。

3. 使用目的

我们只将信息用于身份验证；维护资料、好友关系、屏蔽和隐私选择；在 App 与小组件中显示城市级位置；同步更改；创建并发送符合条件的同城事件；避免重复通知；保护服务安全；以及排查失败操作。

4. 信息共享

  • 已接受的好友：仅能看到你的全局和逐好友设置允许的最近城市及更新时间，无法看到精确坐标。
  • Apple：提供“通过 Apple 登录”和 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）。通知文字可能通过 APNs 传输；你可以在 App 或 iOS 设置中隐藏通知预览。
  • Supabase：为测试服务提供身份验证、数据库、服务器函数和托管基础设施。
  • 法律与安全：在遵守法律、保护用户、调查滥用或保障服务安全确有必要时，我们可能披露相关信息。

我们不会出售或出租个人信息，也不会将其用于跨情境行为广告。

5. 定位、通知与后台访问

定位和通知权限均为可选，并由 iOS 弹窗请求。你可以手动输入城市。授权定位后，App 可以在设备上判断城市；后台刷新机会由 iOS 控制，可能存在延迟。你可以随时暂停全部城市共享、调整逐好友权限、关闭后台更新或通知，或在 iOS 设置中撤销系统权限。

6. 保留期限

  • 新的当前城市记录会替换旧记录；我们不会建立精确坐标或路线历史。
  • 账号、资料、好友关系、偏好、当前城市、设备和同城事件记录会在账号有效期间保留，并在账号删除完成时移除。
  • 已停用的通知注册和未完成删除请求的最小诊断信息，仅在通知可靠性、安全或完成请求所合理需要的期间保留。
  • 已删除数据可能在服务提供商的加密备份中短期存在，之后会随其备份保留周期自然清除。

7. 你的选择与删除账号

你可以编辑资料、暂停城市共享、修改逐好友访问、移除或屏蔽用户、调整小组件隐私，并控制定位、后台和通知权限。

若要删除账号，请在 App 中打开 “你”→“删除账号” 并确认。本服务会删除你的 Supabase Auth 身份及关联资料、好友关系、偏好、当前城市、通知设备、事件以及本地 App/小组件缓存。由于当前测试版不保留 Apple refresh token，你还可以在 Apple 账号的 “登录与安全性”→“通过 Apple 登录” 中单独移除本 App 的授权。如果 App 内删除失败，请通过下方支持链接联系我们。

8. 安全与跨境处理

我们使用传输层 TLS、服务器端鉴权、数据库行级访问控制、加密的 APNs 令牌存储，以及 Apple/Supabase 会话保护。任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。服务提供商可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理信息，并适用其合同与法律保障。

9. 儿童

本服务不面向 13 岁以下儿童，或其所在地区可以自行同意数据处理的最低年龄以下人士。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们删除。

10. 政策变更与联系

我们可能随测试版功能变化更新本政策。我们会更新上方日期；如果变更会实质影响你的权利或信息使用方式，我们还会在 App 中提供额外说明。

如有隐私请求或问题，请访问支持页面，或在不包含私人数据的前提下通过 GitHub Issues 联系。请勿在公开 Issue 中发布身份令牌、精确位置或其他敏感信息。
