Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Last Updated: August 27, 2026

🛡️

Privacy at a Glance

Across Us is designed to share your current city with accepted friends, never a precise GPS trail. Geocoding is resolved entirely on-device. We never sell personal data, never track you across apps, and never serve ads.

1. Scope & Core Principles

This policy covers the Across Us iOS app, Widgets, and supporting backend services. Across Us operates on the principle of mutual consent and minimal data retention.

2. Information We Process

  • Account & Authentication: An anonymous user identifier connected to Sign in with Apple, your chosen display name and username, and avatar palette. We never access your contacts or phone number.
  • City-Level Presence: Coarse city name and country code resolved on-device (e.g., "New York, US"). Precise coordinates used by Apple's CoreLocation stay strictly on your iPhone and are never sent to our servers.
  • Friend Connections & Preferences: Friend requests, accepted friends, per-friend sharing toggles, and notification settings.
  • Notifications: APNs device token for delivering background same-city moments. Device tokens are securely encrypted at rest.

3. How We Use Information

We process information solely to authenticate your session, maintain friend connections, display city presence in widgets, and generate same-city notifications when you and an accepted friend enter the same city.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • Accepted Friends: Receive only your current city name and update timestamp according to your sharing settings.
  • Apple: Provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Push Notification service (APNs).
  • Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase): Encrypted database storage and serverless edge functions.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data in any way.

5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You can pause city sharing globally at any time. You can delete your account and all associated server records with a single tap in You → Delete account.

隐私政策

生效与更新日期：2026 年 8 月 27 日

🛡️

隐私要点速览

Across Us 旨在向双向好友共享你的当前城市，而非实时精准轨迹。地理逆编码完全在 iPhone 端侧完成。我们绝不出售个人数据、绝不跨 App 追踪用户，且无任何广告推广。

1. 适用范围与核心原则

本隐私政策适用于 Across Us iOS App、小组件及后台服务。Across Us 始终遵循“双向同意、极简收集、零精准追踪”原则。

2. 我们处理的信息

  • 账户与身份认证： 关联至 Apple 登录（Sign in with Apple）的匿名标识符、你填写的昵称与用户名、头像配色方案。我们不索取通讯录或手机号。
  • 城市级状态（Presence）： 端侧地理编码解析出的粗粒度城市与国家代码（如 "New York, US"）及更新时间戳。精准 GPS 坐标保留在设备端，绝不上云。
  • 好友关系与偏好： 好友申请、好友列表、单人/全局共享开关及小组件隐私模式。
  • 通知服务： 用于发送同城提醒的加密 APNs Token。

3. 信息的使用目的

所收集的信息仅用于维护账户、好友连接、主屏幕/锁屏小组件展示，以及在好友同城时生成相聚时刻。

4. 信息共享与第三方服务

  • 已接受的好友： 仅能查看你授权共享的城市名及更新时间。
  • Apple： 提供 Apple 登录与 APNs 推送基础设施。
  • 云端服务（Supabase）： 提供加密数据库与无服务器计算支持。

我们绝不出售、出租任何个人数据，亦不用于广告投放。

5. 你的权利与一键注销

你可随时全局或针对单独好友暂停共享。在 App 的 You（个人）→ Delete account（注销账号） 中，可一键彻底删除全部账号与云端记录。
