Designed for Calm Sociality

No endless chat groups, no precise tracking, zero social anxiety.

🏙️

City-Level Presence

Only coarse city names are shared with mutual friends. Precise GPS coordinates are reverse-geocoded entirely on-device and never sent to our servers.

📱

Lock Screen & Widgets

Glance at your closest friends’ whereabouts right from your Home Screen and Lock Screen with small, medium, and circular widgets.

✨

Same-City Moments

When you and a friend's travel paths cross in the same city, Across Us celebrates the reunion with an ongoing shared moment and single alert.

🔒

Sign in with Apple

One-tap authentication with Apple. No phone numbers, no address book uploads, and immediate one-tap account deletion whenever you choose.

🔋

Opportunistic & Low Energy

Uses Apple’s Significant Location Changes and Visits APIs. Zero battery-draining continuous GPS polling in the background.

👁️

Granular Privacy Modes

Pause city sharing globally anytime, or customize visibility per-friend. Choose to hide names or details on your Lock Screen widget.
